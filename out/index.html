How I build with AI

AI multiplies output. Judgment decides where it goes.

I use AI agents every day, as leverage, not as autopilot. This is the workflow behind Tiny Tarrasque, and it's the one I'd bring to your team.

01

Agents with guardrails

Every workspace carries its own CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md with hard rules, specialised sub-agents own the rules engine and the mobile app, and a verify gate (typecheck plus the full test suite) has to pass before anything counts as done.

02

Small, reviewable steps

One branch and one PR per task, CI on every PR, decisions recorded as ADRs and a short hand-off note between sessions. AI writes a lot of the code; I own the architecture, the review and the merge.

03

A team's pace, solo

In six weeks: a game-agnostic rules engine, two rule systems shipped as data and a cross-platform app, in 110+ commits and 85 test files.

04

AI in the product, responsibly

The planned AI backstory feature runs through my own server so no API key ships in the app, sits behind a fair-use cap, and never replaces the offline generator that stays the default.
