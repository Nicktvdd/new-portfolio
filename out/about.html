Hey there! 👋

I'm Nick, a Dutch full-stack engineer living in Helsinki with my Finnish wife Sonja. We're here for the nature and the people, and we love going on adventures together.

I took the scenic route into software. I studied sport science, booked bands for music venues, and spent years as a wilderness guide and instructor in Lapland. Then I learned to code at Hive Helsinki.

At Soil Scout I went from Full Stack Engineer to Lead Full Stack Engineer, leading development of The Hub, the dashboard for our buried soil sensors. In June the company moved on to a different product and my role ended, so now I'm building something of my own: Tiny Tarrasque, an app for people who play tabletop role-playing games together.

Health still runs my day: gym, good food, meditation, and lately skateboarding again. Now I'm looking for my next team, ideally as a CTO or founding engineer. If you're building something with great people, let's talk. ✨
